How I approach a prototype

From workflow idea to reviewable demo

Designed to make requirements easier to discuss before anyone commits to a production build.

Understand the workflow

List the stock, expiry, supplier, and reporting tasks to demonstrate.

Shape the screens

Group the common actions into a compact, easy-to-follow interface.

Build the demo

Connect sample forms, lists, and reports for a guided walkthrough.

Review and refine

Use feedback to clarify the next scope and production requirements.

Prototype scope

Made for demonstrations and workflow planning

This portfolio project is a browser-based prototype with sample data. It can help a pharmacy team discuss screens and requirements before a secure production system is planned.

Not for live pharmacy operations.
This demo does not provide production-grade authentication, a secure shared database, hosting, regulatory certification, or protection for real patient or business records. Do not enter real data.
